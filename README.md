# Unilag Campus Life

Build unilaglife.app, a web-based multiplayer campus life simulator and social game set in the University of Lagos (UNILAG) inspired by lagoslife.app.

Key features and systems:
- Scaled bird's-eye interactive campus map featuring UNILAG landmarks and hubs: Main Gate, University Road, Senate Building, New Hall, Sports Centre, Akintunde Ojo Main Library, Faculties (Science, Engineering, Law, Arts, Medilag), Hostels (Jaja, Mariere, Moremi, Amina, Eni Njoku, Biobaku, Kofo, etc.), and Lagoon Front, plus bordering off-campus zones (Abule-Oja, Akoka).
- Transportation network with campus shuttles, keke napep, and walking mechanics with stamina and time costs.
- Student profile onboarding: Faculty, Department, Level (100L-500L), Matric/student email verification, and Residence (Hostel hall/room or Off-Campus).
- Social and hostel living layer: Explore hostels, view real students in rooms, visit, knock on doors, send notes, invite roommates to squat/cohabit, and customize bunk/room spaces.
- Campus economy and hubs: New Hall food joints (Korede Spag, Iya Moria amala, suya), campus businesses (POS kiosk, cybercafe, print shop, hair salon), and clickable campus billboards/noticeboards where students promote gigs and businesses.
- Campus politics and governance: SUG and Hall Executive elections, manifestos, and voting.
- Academic and survival stats: CGPA meter, energy, hunger, sanity, lecture attendance tactics, CBT exam minigames, TDB night reading, and random UNILAG events (ASUU strikes, hostel water wahala, gate security checks).
- Campus events and radio: Hall week concerts, money/meal-voucher spraying, and an embedded UNILAG 103.1 FM campus radio stream.
